Cover the Square

Drag the rectangles onto the square until they tile it perfectly. Click (or tap) a piece to rotate it 90°.

How to play

Setup

  • Square size — cells per side, up to 9.
  • Auto-generate — pick a piece count (and how many start locked as "starters"), then Generate. Pieces are cut straight out of the square, so it's always solvable.
  • Manual shapes — type one piece per line: h, w, locked. "locked" is optional — colors are assigned automatically. Add locked@x-y instead of locked to pin a piece to an exact top-left cell (0-indexed). Piece areas must add up to size².

Playing

  • Drag a piece from the tray onto the grid — it snaps to the nearest cell.
  • Click a piece without dragging it to rotate it 90°.
  • Click a placed piece's corner badge to lock 🔒 or unlock 🔓 it, so it won't move by accident. Starter pieces begin locked — unlock one to pick it up.
  • Pieces in the tray can't overlap each other — dropping one on top of another snaps it back to where it started.
  • Stuck? Compute solution checks whether the board is still solvable given whatever's currently locked, then Reveal solution shows where every remaining piece goes.

Win by covering every cell of the square with no gaps or overlaps.
